## What we do

Rise Up Ecom supports brands selling on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Nykaa and other marketplaces. We help organize the everyday work behind an online store, from getting products listed to reviewing advertising and account performance.

- **Marketplace onboarding:** support with seller setup, documentation and catalogue preparation.
- **Account management:** ongoing seller operations, account health monitoring and reporting.
- **Product listings:** catalogue updates, content improvement and listing optimization.
- **Advertising:** campaign setup, performance reviews and optimization.
- **Growth planning:** practical priorities based on products, inventory and business goals.

## A practical checklist for marketplace sellers

1. Keep product titles, images, specifications and stock information accurate.
2. Review pending orders, dispatch deadlines and seller notifications regularly.
3. Measure advertising costs alongside returns, fees and product margins.
4. Give every operational task a clear owner.
5. Review results weekly and agree on the next actions.
